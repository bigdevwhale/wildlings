# Privacy

Wildling has no account, no ads and no analytics, and it doesn't ask for internet access at all. Your habits, days, cravings, journal notes, letter and creatures stay on your phone, in the app's own storage. Android's backup may keep a copy, as with any app, if you turned it on. A copy you save from the settings is an ordinary file: it lies where you put it and goes only where you send it.

The evening question is a local notification; nothing is sent to show it.

Postcards leave the phone only when you share them yourself.

Lingvale, the village of our pet apps, can read the creatures and the days through a small read-only bridge. It answers only Lingvale signed with our key, changes nothing, and the data still never leaves the phone.
