## [1.3.0] - 2026-11-18

- **Lingvale can move Wildling to a new phone.** The village saves the copies of all the -ling apps into one file and loads them back on another phone. It is the same copy Settings → Data makes, and Wildling loads it only when you confirm it in Lingvale. A file from a newer Wildling is refused, as in the settings.

## [1.2.0] - 2026-11-18

- **A copy in a file.** Settings → Data saves everything the app keeps – habits, days, cravings, the journal, the letter, the lodge and the garden – as one file, and reads it back. Move it to a new phone or keep it just in case. ([#4](https://github.com/bigdevwhale/wildlings/issues/4))
- Restoring replaces what is in the app, so it first shows what the file holds and asks. A file that isn't a Wildling copy changes nothing.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-17

- **Counted habits.** A good habit can now be counted in amounts instead of ticked: «5 glasses of water a day» or «1000 words a week». Put in how much was done each day – by steps or typed – and the day (or the week) counts once the goal adds up. A weekly amount brings the creature closer in proportion: half the words, half the week's steps. ([#1](https://github.com/bigdevwhale/wildlings/issues/1))
- The creature's page shows today's amount and the total, and its two-week calendar shows each day's amount; tap a day to put in or fix how much it was.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-17

The first release.

- **Habits as creatures.** A bad habit is a wild beast you tame by not feeding it; a good one is a shy creature you lure by doing it again and again. Trust is worked out from the days themselves: a slip takes a step back, never back to zero.
- **48 invented creatures:** 12 wild and 12 shy for ready habits, 11 of each for habits you name, 4 rare guests.
- **The lodge:** a yard at the forest's edge and a house cut open with four rooms; the sky follows the hour; garden beds and things bought with pinecones.
- **«It came»:** a four-minute sitting by the window with the beast, breathing, small things to do, your reason and a letter to yourself.
- **The journal trail** with the weather inside, milestones on the way, the map of hard days and the cravings: the beast's clock, the sea of waves, causes and what helps.
- **The chronicle** in four tabs, and postcards to share.
