## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-17

The first release.

- **Habits as creatures.** A bad habit is a wild beast you tame by not feeding it; a good one is a shy creature you lure by doing it again and again. Trust is worked out from the days themselves: a slip takes a step back, never back to zero.
- **48 invented creatures:** 12 wild and 12 shy for ready habits, 11 of each for habits you name, 4 rare guests.
- **The lodge:** a yard at the forest's edge and a house cut open with four rooms; the sky follows the hour; garden beds and things bought with pinecones.
- **«It came»:** a four-minute sitting by the window with the beast, breathing, small things to do, your reason and a letter to yourself.
- **The journal trail** with the weather inside, milestones on the way, the map of hard days and the cravings: the beast's clock, the sea of waves, causes and what helps.
- **The chronicle** in four tabs, and postcards to share.
